Sodexo RevUP

Redesigning Invoice Reconciliation for a B2B Finance Platform

Product Overview

RevUP is Sodexo's internal B2B SaaS finance platform built for invoice reconciliation. Finance teams rely on it to align billing data across complex multi-month cycles — reconciling invoices, tracking supplemental charges, and auditing historical records across enterprise accounts.

The existing workflow created bottlenecks that required manual intervention at nearly every step, generating significant overhead for billing teams handling high volumes of accounts each cycle.

The Work

The design engagement covered five core capability areas, each targeting a distinct pain point in the reconciliation lifecycle:

  • 13-Month Invoice Visibility: Surfaced a rolling 13-month invoice view, giving finance teams the context needed to identify discrepancies without switching tools or requesting data exports.
  • Auto 6-Month Catch-Up: Designed an automated catch-up mechanism that reconciles up to six months of billing gaps in a single workflow — eliminating the manual backfill process that previously consumed entire billing cycles.
  • Supplemental Month Control: Created controls for managing supplemental billing months, allowing finance managers to adjust scope without re-opening base invoices or disrupting downstream approvals.
  • VDA Calculation & Invoice Alignment: Built the interface for volume discount agreement (VDA) calculations and tied them directly to invoice alignment, ensuring discount-adjusted totals propagate correctly across related records.
  • 7-Year Invoice History: Designed a structured archive experience giving teams auditable access to seven years of invoice history — supporting compliance reviews and dispute resolution without relying on offline records.

Results

85% faster per billing cycle after the redesign
202 hours returned to finance teams annually
5 wks of FTE capacity freed per year

The redesigned platform dramatically reduced manual intervention in the reconciliation cycle. Teams that previously spent the majority of each billing period on data alignment were able to redirect that time to higher-value financial analysis and exception handling.
