Mobile Design

Learning on the Go

A mobile-first study solution for students

Project Overview

Pearson Prep is a mobile-first solution to help students study. By bringing together a student's course and study materials — both author-provided and student-created — giving them a place to start, and enabling them to target what they need to learn most when they can learn it best.

Process

Involved in ideation, research, and validation phases, I worked with PM, Learning Design, and a Prototyper to create working demos for research. Then moved forward with production and continued to improve and iterate through product cycles.

Timeline

Mobile: iOS & Android — Available in the Apple App Store & Google Play Store.

Desktop version released in Q4 2019.

Product timeline from kickoff to launch.

Key Features

  • Pre-made Expert Decks
  • Auto-create flashcards from class notes
  • Manual create individual flashcards
  • Adaptive practice

Site Maps

In order to make sense of the product criteria and features, sitemap design helped the product team, stakeholders, and developers get an overview and understand the scope of effort. With the sitemap approved, we translated it into UI screens to ensure the functions were properly represented.

Sitemap helps dev and product team understand the general flow.

Results

One initial key metric was getting to a live working mobile & desktop app for a 2,000-person beta test in 10 weeks.

13.2k subscribers in the first 3 months
$26k revenue from premium decks purchased
10 wk from kickoff to live beta for 2,000 users

The Prep product was a success — it validated that students desired additional study content and were willing to pay for it if it was high quality. The expert decks were subsequently rolled into the Pearson Plus version of eText. This was the first app for Pearson Higher Ed to release in the app store and generate revenue.
